TOGBankClassic - Guild Bank Management for Classic Era & TBC

Are you tired of logging into multiple bank alts to find items? Does your guild rely on character banks instead of the guild vault? TOGBankClassic is the solution!

TOGBankClassic is a powerful, feature-rich addon that transforms how your guild manages inventory across multiple bank characters. View, search, and request items from all your guild bank alts without ever logging out—all in one convenient interface.

Core Features

One Guild Bank Window

  • One-Click Access: the minimap button opens the Guild Bank window — every bank character's items as one sortable list, with four tabs: Browse, Bankers, Requests and Log
  • No Alt-Hopping: Never log out to check what's in stock—see everything at a glance
  • Real-Time Updates: Inventory automatically syncs across all guild members using the addon
  • Mail Integration: Track items in transit—what is waiting in a bank character's mailbox counts as part of the total
  • Item Tooltip: Hover any item anywhere in-game to see which bankers have it and their exact stock levels—no addon window needed
  • Bankers Tab: one row per bank character — online or not, what it stores (from its guild note), whether your copy is current, when it last published, and how much it holds. Click one to browse just that bank
  • Bank Log: what has moved in and out of the guild bank, newest first — deposits, withdrawals, money, and every request as it is placed, filled, cancelled or reopened, with who was on each side where the addon can tell
  • Remembers Itself: where you put the window, how big you made it and which tab you were on, through reloads and logouts
  • The old tab-per-character Inventory window is still there as /togbank legacy while the new one settles

Powerful Search and Filters

  • Guild-Wide Search: one search box across every bank character — every word you type has to appear somewhere in the row
  • Filters Across The Top: bank, type, subtype, slot, quality, level range and Usable by me (hides anything your character cannot use — wrong class, race, armour or weapon type, or too high a level); they apply as you change them, and Clear puts them all back
  • Sortable Columns: click any column heading — name, quantity, banker, type, required level — to sort by it; click again to reverse
  • Banker Column: which bank character holds the item, with a dot for that bank's status (green current, red behind, yellow an update on its way)
  • Click to Request: click any row to open a request dialog with smart quantity suggestions
  • Availability Info: See max requestable amounts based on officer-configured limits
  • Full Names: items with a random suffix ("Spiked Club of the Bear") show their full name everywhere, so two variants never read as one

Smart Request System

  • In-Game Requests: Guild members request items directly through the addon—no whispers needed
  • Request Tracking: View all pending, fulfilled, and completed orders on the Requests tab, in the same striped-row style as the rest of the window, with one search box that matches the date, requester, bank or item
  • Timeline Tooltips: Hover any request's date to see when it was submitted, filled, or cancelled (with reason)
  • Cancel Reasons: Bankers select a reason before cancelling; reason is stored and shown to the requester, and a cancelled request's date glows gently so you know to look
  • Out-Of-Date Requesters: a request from a guildmate whose addon is too old to see the current bank contents shows their version beside their name, so a banker knows why an item may not be where they expect
  • Requests Archive: Requests older than a configurable threshold automatically move to an Archive tab, keeping the main view focused
  • Stale Request Management: orders left unfilled past your chosen age close automatically, and officers can clear them in one go with the "Cancel Stale" button
  • Priority System: Mark requests as Rush, High, Normal, or Low priority for better organization
  • Officer Controls: Officers can configure guild-wide request percentage limits (1-100% of inventory)

Automated Order Fulfillment

  • One-Click Fulfill: Bankers click "Fulfill" button—addon automatically attaches items to mail
  • Collect From The Bank: the same button works at the bank — each click pulls out what an order still needs, splitting oversized stacks and putting the spare straight back. Gather at the bank, walk to the mailbox, keep clicking to send
  • Smart Stack Splitting: Automatically splits oversized stacks with confirmation dialogs
    • Example: Request for 2 Felcloth from a 5-stack? Addon prompts to split precisely 2 items
    • Complex requests (e.g., 175 Runecloth from mixed stacks) handled automatically
  • Optimal Stack Selection: Intelligent bin-packing algorithm selects best stack combinations
  • Visual Indicators: Button icons change to show split needed vs. ready to mail
  • One Mail Per Person: several orders for the same guildmate go in one mail — click Fulfill on each and they are added to the mail already open for them, then Send once
  • Fulfill Oldest: one envelope button that works the queue oldest-first — at the mailbox it picks the oldest order you can fill, adds that person's other orders, does every stack split at once, attaches and sends; at the bank the same button collects what the open orders need, swapping unneeded stacks into the bank when your bags are full
  • Mailbox Window: a striped-row view of your inbox that opens beside the mail frame — one row per mail, click to see each attachment with its tooltip, filter by item, sender or subject, take a needed item with a click, Take Needed to collect only what the open orders are short of, and an envelope on each row to return a mail to its sender
  • Stay-Online Line: after filling orders, the bottom bar of every window tells a bank character whether the update has reached anyone yet — red until it has been sent, amber once someone has it, green when a guildmate confirms — so nobody logs off leaving the guild seeing stock that is already gone

Blazing-Fast Synchronization

  • Bank contents travel as compact numbers, not item links. Your own client rebuilds each item from its ID, so nothing is sent that your game already knows — measured at 85% less data than the old format across 227 items on a live guild.
  • Nothing is sent when nothing changed. A bank character's contents get a version stamp when they are scanned — only when something actually changed, so opening the bank to look costs nothing — and members compare stamps before asking for anything. A stamp says when it was published, so "who has the newest copy" is one glance.
  • Seconds, not minutes. When a bank character's new version is announced — on the ten-minute check-in, when they open the Inventory window or type /togbank share, or the moment they hide or show an item — everyone online who is behind on it has the update within seconds; the announcement itself is the offer.
  • Only what changed. Catching up on a bank you already hold sends just the few things that moved since your copy, checked against the bank character's own fingerprint before it is applied — a fraction of the size of the whole bank. Someone who has never seen that bank, or has been away a long time, still gets the full contents.
  • Tiny messages. Every bank character has a permanent four-digit number, the same on everyone's screen, so a guild-wide check-in is a few hundred bytes and a "I have newer copies of these" reply is a single message however many banks it covers.
  • No single bottleneck. Data comes from whichever guild member has the freshest copy, not from one designated player who has to be online. Downloads from different guildmates run at the same time, and the short back-and-forth that sets them up travels on a fast lane of its own, so it is answered even while the other player is busy sending to someone else.
  • Nobody is refused. A player sends to three people at a time; anyone else is told their place in the queue and waits their turn, or asks someone else who has the same bank.
  • The colour tells you where you stand. On the Bankers tab each bank's Status is green Current, red Behind (a newer copy exists and is on its way), Old format (that bank has not published on this version yet), grey No data or Newer copy unreachable (the only newer copy is on a guildmate whose addon is too old to send it); the old Inventory window's tabs are coloured the same way. Hover for who said what.
  • Log in and catch up. Anything you missed while offline arrives shortly after you log in, without you asking.
  • Built to stay quiet. Traffic is queued and paced so a busy guild chat doesn't get flooded and messages don't arrive garbled. In a raid group it pauses altogether, so raid addons have the chat channel to themselves — the bottom bar says so, and a setting lets you keep syncing anyway.
  • Shared accounts handled. A bank character played from more than one computer never publishes a stale vault over the guild's newer copy: that computer's own tab goes red, says what to open, and publishes only once it has caught up.
  • Nothing to configure. All of this runs on its own; the one sync setting is the raid pause.

Advanced Features

For Guild Members

  • Minimap Button: Quick access to the Guild Bank window—toggle visibility in options
  • Bag Highlighting: items needed for open orders are marked in your bags — works with the default bags, Bagnon, ElvUI and Baganator
  • Request History: View your past requests with fulfillment status
  • Fair Distribution: Officer-configured limits ensure equitable access to guild resources
  • Window Persistence: All window sizes and positions save automatically across reloads, per character; a window that has ended up off screen comes back with Recenter All Windows under Settings > Appearance
  • Visual Consistency: every window shares the same title, bottom status bar, thin scrollbars and borders, and a ? beside Close that explains it — on the Guild Bank window it pulses gently until you have hovered it once on each tab
  • Mute Warnings: Checkbox to hide low-priority warnings for cleaner chat

For Bank Alts

  • Automatic Detection: Add "gbank" to Public Note or Officer Note—addon handles the rest. Write what the bank keeps beside it ("gbank herbs, potions") and it shows in the Bankers tab's Stores column
  • Auto-Scan: Configure to scan bank/bags/mail automatically when opened
  • Mail Fulfillment: Receive requests, click Fulfill, send mail—streamlined 3-step process, with the recipient filled in for you
  • Bank Collection: click the same Fulfill button at your bank to pull out what open orders need before you head to the mailbox — and when your bags are full, it swaps something no order needs into the bank to make room
  • Hide Items From The Guild: right-click any item on your own tab to hide it — your hearthstone, stock you are holding back, old gear. It stays on your tab greyed with a red mark; to everyone else it is as if you do not have it. Right-click again to show it. A Bank setting hides every soulbound item at once
  • Numbered Automatically: every bank character gets a permanent four-digit number the first time its bank is scanned — nothing to type, and it shows beside the name in /togbank roster
  • Donation Tracking: Optional feature to accept and track donations from guild members; a mailed donation is logged as a deposit, naming the sender, when you take it out of the mail
  • Know Before You Log Off: the bottom bar tells you whether your latest update has reached the guild, and warns you in chat if you start the logout countdown while it has not

For Officers

  • Request Limits: Set max request percentage (e.g., 50% = members can request up to half of available stock)
  • Protects Unique Items: Always allows requesting at least 1 if available (gear, BoE epics, etc.)
  • Syncs Guild-Wide: your settings reach everyone online straight away, and anyone who was offline picks them up when they next log in — you only set them once
  • Auto-Cancel Threshold: choose how many days an unfilled order stays open before it is closed as stale
  • Custom Cancel Reasons: write your own reasons for cancelling an order, and choose which ones members and bankers each see
  • Help Notes: add a note of your own to each window's help tooltip, so guild-specific rules are visible where people need them

If Something Goes Wrong

  • Built-in logging. Turn it on with /togbank debug, send it to its own chat tab with /togbank debugtab, and grab a copy for a bug report with /togbank debuglog. Off by default, so it costs you nothing when you're not using it.
  • Update notifications. You're told when a newer version is out, so you're not silently running an old one while your guild has moved on.
  • Diagnostics on request. If a problem needs digging into, there are extra tools behind /togbank dev that support can walk you through on Discord.

How It Works

Setup (One-Time)

  1. Bank Characters: Add "gbank" to character's Public Note or Officer Note
  2. Check It Is Enabled: In AddOns settings, open the Bank section and make sure Enable for <character> is ticked. That is the only setting that governs whether this character's bank is scanned and shared, and it is on by default — so usually there is nothing to do here.
  3. Scan Inventory: Open your bank and then close it — the scan runs on close. The guild picks it up on the next ten-minute check-in, or straight away if you type /togbank share
  4. Done! Inventory is now visible to all guild members using TOGBankClassic

Daily Usage

For Guild Members:

  1. Click the minimap button to open the Guild Bank window
  2. Type in the search box (e.g., "Felcloth"), or narrow the list with the filters across the top
  3. Click the row → dialog shows available quantity
  4. Enter amount → click "Send Request"
  5. Wait for mail notification—it's that easy!

For Bank Alts:

  1. Open the Guild Bank window → the Requests tab (it opens there by itself when you click the mail frame's Send Mail tab on a bank character)
  2. See all pending requests with quantities
  3. Click "Fulfill" on any request — or the Fulfill Oldest envelope to work the queue oldest-first
  4. Addon attaches items (auto-splits if needed) and fills in the recipient
  5. Send mail → done! Several orders for one person go in the same mail

Slash Commands

Everyday Commands

  • Minimap button — open or close the Guild Bank window (shift-click for the options)
  • /togbank or /togbank legacy — open or close the old Inventory window, a tab per bank character
  • /togbank mailbox — open the Mailbox window while you are at a mailbox (it opens by itself for bank characters; there is also a TOG Bank button on the mail frame's title bar)
  • /togbank help — list every available command
  • /togbank roster — list the bank characters with their four-digit numbers
  • /togbank sync — manually pull the latest data from your guild (automatic every ten minutes)
  • /togbank share — announce your own bank's current version to the guild now (automatic every ten minutes, whenever you open the Inventory window, and the moment you hide or show an item)
  • /togbank version — show which version you are running
  • /togbank wipeframes — forget every saved window position and size: open windows recentre at once, sizes go back to default on the next reload. For a window that is simply off screen, Recenter All Windows under Settings > Appearance moves it without touching sizes
  • /togbank helpreset — forget which help icons you have hovered, so they pulse again

/bank and /gbank can be enabled as shortcuts in the options, if nothing else has claimed them.

Resetting Data

  • /togbank wipe — clear this guild's stored data (it re-syncs from your guild afterwards)
  • /togbank wipeall — clear everything and start fresh

Troubleshooting

  • /togbank debug — turn debug messages on or off
  • /togbank debugtab — send debug messages to their own chat tab instead of cluttering yours
  • /togbank debuglog — print a recent slice of the log to paste into a bug report

There are further diagnostic commands behind /togbank dev, intended for development and support. /togbank dev help lists them; you should not need them in normal play.

Configuration

Access Options

  • Press ESC → Options → AddOns → TOGBankClassic
  • Or right-click the minimap button

Key Settings

  • General: Minimap button, hide in combat, chat message volume, sounds, and Keep syncing in a raid group (off by default)
  • Appearance: A transparency slider for each window, Reset All Windows to Solid, Recenter All Windows for a window lost off screen, and Pulsing glow on cancelled requests (on by default)
  • Bank (bank characters only): Enable for <character> — the one switch that governs whether this bank is scanned and shared — Hide soulbound items from the guild, and the donation options
  • Officer: Request limits and how long requests stay open
  • Debug: Persistent logging and debug categories

Dependencies & Compatibility

Required

All of these install automatically through the CurseForge app — you do not need to fetch them yourself.

  • Ace3 — core framework
  • VersionCheck-1.0 — tells you when a new version is out
  • LibGuildRoster — guild roster and online/offline tracking
  • AceCommQueue — keeps addon traffic orderly so nothing arrives garbled
  • ItemDB — the offline item database bank contents are named from, suffixes included
  • DeltaSync — the shared guild-sync engine
  • LibAceGUIWidgets — the shared search box, date picker and window widgets

Optional — bag addons

The "Highlight needed items" option works with the default Blizzard bags and with the bag addons below. The look differs depending on which one you use — that is a limit of what each addon lets others change, not a bug:

  • Bagnon — dims the items you don't need. Limited to the first 20 distinct items.
  • ElvUI — dims the items you don't need, in both the bag and bank windows, using ElvUI's own shading.
  • Baganator — puts a gold marker on the items you do need. Nothing is dimmed, because corner markers are the only thing Baganator lets other addons add.

Compatibility

  • World of Warcraft Classic Era (Interface 11509)
  • World of Warcraft The Burning Crusade (Interface 20506)
  • Works alongside other guild management addons
  • Other bag addons are not interfered with, but item highlighting only supports the four listed above — everything else in the addon works normally regardless of which bag addon you run

Works With the Rest of the TOG Addons

TOG Profession Master

If your guild also runs TOG Profession Master, the two talk to each other automatically — there is nothing to set up on this side beyond having TOGBankClassic loaded.

  • Crafters can see guild bank stock without leaving the profession window. A [Bank] button appears next to any reagent your bankers actually hold — in the recipe breakdown, the cooldown panel, shopping lists and the Reagent Tracker. If nothing is in stock, no button.
  • They can request reagents from there too. Click [Bank], pick which banker to ask, choose a quantity, and the order arrives in TOGBankClassic like any other request — including your officer-configured limits.
  • No duplicate bookkeeping. TOG Profession Master reads the same stock figures this addon already keeps, so there is only ever one set of numbers to trust.

TOG Tools

If you also run TOG Tools, its Logs > Guild Bank tab — empty on Classic Era on its own, because Era has no guild bank — fills in with this addon's bank log: every bank character's deposits, withdrawals and money, and every request as it is placed, filled, cancelled or reopened, as they happen. TOG Tools keeps the long history; this addon keeps only the most recent entries for its own Log tab. Nothing to set up on either side.

Shared Foundations

The TOG addons are built on a common set of libraries rather than each reinventing the same plumbing:

  • DeltaSync — the guild-sync engine that keeps data current across members
  • LibGuildRoster — guild roster and online/offline tracking
  • AceCommQueue — keeps addon traffic orderly so nothing arrives garbled
  • ItemDB — an offline item database, so item names and details work without waiting on the game to load them
  • LibAceGUIWidgets — the search box, date picker, pulsing highlights and window-position memory the TOG windows share, so they all look and behave alike
  • VersionCheck — tells you when a newer version is out

In practice that means a fix to the shared sync layer benefits every TOG addon at once, and running several of them costs less than the sum of their parts. Each is published separately and installs automatically through the CurseForge app.

Recent Updates

v1.5.0 - Setting The Red

Everyone in your guild should update. Bank contents no longer travel between this version and older ones: a guildmate still on v1.4.1 stops receiving bank contents from anyone who has updated, and is not asked for theirs. Nothing breaks for them — requests, the roster and everything else still work across versions — they simply stop seeing current bank contents until they update, and a banker sees their requests marked with the old version number. One thing to do once after updating, bank characters: open and close your bank once, so its contents are published in the new form; that bank's status may go red for guildmates for a moment while the fresh copy reaches them.

New

  • The addon is now called TOG Bank on screen. Every window is titled that way; nothing else changes, and you do not need to do anything.
  • The Guild Bank window remembers where you put it, how big you made it, and which tab you were last on — through closing and reopening it, and through logging out. It cannot be made narrower than its Requests tab needs, so the column headings stay inside the frame. And it keeps itself up to date while open: the Bankers tab and the status dots redraw as bank contents arrive, and the Online column changes the moment a bank character logs in or out.
  • A Recenter All Windows button, under Settings > Appearance. If a window has ended up off the edge of your screen where you cannot grab its title bar to drag it back, this brings every window to the middle of the screen straight away. Window sizes are left alone.
  • A setting to keep syncing while you are in a raid group. Guild bank data is normally neither sent nor received in a raid, so raid addons have the chat channel to themselves — tick Keep syncing in a raid group under Settings > General if you would rather it carried on. Off unless you turn it on.
  • A new Guild Bank window that shows every banker's items as one list instead of a tab per character. Click the minimap button, or press Browse on the old Inventory window (/togbank legacy opens that older window while it lasts). Filters run across the top — search, bank, type, subtype, slot, quality, level range, usable by me — and narrow the list as you change them; the rows below are sortable by any column and show the item's name in its quality colour, how many, which banker holds it (with a dot for that bank's status), its type and required level. Click a row to request it. A Bankers tab lists each bank character with whether they are online, what they store (whatever is written in their guild note beside the gbank marker — "gbank herbs, potions"), whether your copy is current, when they last published, and how much they hold; its search box matches the name, what they store and the status, and clicking a row shows just that bank. A Requests tab holds the requests list itself — the same list, search, filters and buttons as the Requests window, inside this one. A Log tab shows what has moved in and out of the guild bank, newest first — deposits and withdrawals as each bank character publishes a rescan, and every request as it is placed, mailed, handed over, cancelled or reopened — with who, what, how many and, when known, who it went to or came from; mouse over an item for its tooltip. Across its top: a search box (a name shows that person's entries whether they were giving or receiving; an item or an action works too) and Since / Until date pickers — click the field for a small calendar, or type YYYY-MM-DD. Press Clear on the Browse tab to put every filter back. Usable by me hides anything your character cannot use — the wrong class, race, armour or weapon type, or too high a level. The ? next to Close explains each tab; it pulses gently until the first time you hover it on that tab — Browse, Bankers, Requests and Log each explain themselves differently — then stays still. The gear beside it opens the addon's settings, as on the main window. The old Inventory and Search windows are unchanged — this is the way in now, and they remain while it settles.
  • Bank characters can hide items from the guild. On your own tab of the main window, right-click any item to hide it: it stays on your tab greyed out with a red mark, and to everyone else — every guildmate's copy of your bank, the Search window, item tooltips, requests — it is as if you do not have it. Right-click it again to show it. Your hearthstone, stock you are holding to resell, the soulbound odds and ends of a character that used to be a main: keep them without the guild seeing them. The change reaches the guild right away, and works whether or not you are standing at the bank. One thing to know: to the bank log, hiding an item looks like a withdrawal and showing it again looks like a deposit.
  • Bank characters can also hide every soulbound item at once: tick Hide soulbound items from the guild in the Bank settings (the panel bank characters get under the addon's options). Handy for a character that used to be a main and still carries its old gear. Off unless you turn it on; untick it and everything soulbound is shown again, while anything you hid by hand stays hidden.
  • A Mailbox window, in the same striped-row style as the Guild Bank window. For bank characters it opens beside the normal mail frame when you open a mailbox; anyone can open it at any time while at a mailbox with the new TOG Bank button at the left end of the mail frame's title bar, or with /togbank mailbox. Each mail is one row — its subject, who sent it, how many things are attached, how long it has left, and a green needed when a pending request to you wants something on it. Click a mail to open it out into one row per attachment: the icon, the item's link, the quantity, and the game's own tooltip when you mouse over it (mousing over the mail row lists everything on it without opening it). Type in the filter box to narrow by item, sender or subject: mails with nothing that matches disappear, and an opened mail shows only the attachments that match. The clicks are the game's own inbox's: click an item to take it into your bags, shift-click (or right-click the mail) to take everything on that mail, money included. Take Needed takes only the attachments your open orders are still short of — what the orders ask for beyond what is already in your bags — oldest mail first, and leaves everything else where it is; Take Shown collects everything the filter left, one after another. A small envelope at the right of each mail row returns that whole mail to whoever sent it, everything still attached (mail from the game itself — auction house, system mail — has nobody to return to and shows none). Cash-on-delivery mail is marked and left for the normal mail frame. The window remembers where you put it and how big you made it, per character, like the Guild Bank window.
  • A search box on the Requests tab, beside the Requester and Bank dropdowns. Type part of a name to find someone's requests, part of an item to find what is being asked for, or a date to jump to a day — every word you type has to appear somewhere in the row's date, requester, bank or item, and it works on top of the dropdowns. Press the little X to clear it. Every window's search box is the same one, so they all look and behave alike. TOGBankClassic now needs the LibAceGUIWidgets library, which CurseForge installs alongside it.
  • A bank log, in the shape of the game's own guild bank log: one line per thing that happened — which bank character deposited or withdrew what and how many, money in and out, and every request placed, mailed, handed over, cancelled or reopened — recorded by every guildmate's client from the same changes, so everyone's log agrees. Where the addon can tell, a line also names the guildmate on the other side: a withdrawal that filled someone's request names the requester, and a mailed donation is logged as a deposit, naming who sent it, when the bank character takes it out of the mail — not while it sits unopened in the inbox, since it may be sent back. The bank character's own client works that out once, and everyone else's log shows the same rows. TOGBankClassic keeps only the most recent 250 entries per guild — enough for the Log tab to be useful straight after a reload — and offers the feed to other addons to store the long history. If you also run TOGTools, its Logs > Guild Bank tab — empty on Classic Era until now, because Era has no guild bank — fills in with your bank characters' deposits, withdrawals and money, and every request event, as they happen, automatically. Nothing to set up. To watch the log on its own, tick LOG on the Debug tab of the addon's settings: it reports each entry as it is recorded, why a bank update produced none, and each batch as it is handed to the Log tab and to TOGTools.
  • Bank characters can now see whether their latest update has reached the guild before logging off. After you fill orders and close the mailbox (or the bank), the bottom bar of every TOGBank window shows a red line — "Bank update not received by anyone yet — stay online", with how many guildmates are on and how long it has been. It turns amber once the update has been sent to someone, and green — naming the count, with a chat line saying it is safe to log off — only when a guildmate has confirmed they received it. While it is red or amber it breathes gently and takes the whole bar if the window is narrow, so it is never hidden. If nobody in the guild is online to receive it, the line says so in grey instead; the update goes out again at your next login. A change made in the first minute after logging in is held for up to a minute or so while the addon checks with the guild — the bar says so in amber ("Bank update stored, not published yet") until it goes out, and a held update survives a logout or reload, still counting from when it started. Start the logout countdown while the line is still red and a chat warning reminds you. Until now there was no way to tell, and logging off too soon left everyone seeing stock you no longer had until your next session.

Changed

  • You are now told when being in a raid group has paused syncing. Nothing is sent or received while you are in a raid, and until now nothing said so — the addon simply looked as though the guild had gone quiet. You get one line in chat the first time it pauses, and every window's bottom bar reads "Sync paused: in a raid group" for as long as it lasts. It resumes on its own when you leave, or you can switch the pause off in the settings.
  • Collecting for orders at the bank no longer stops when your bags are full. The same click now swaps something in your bags that no order needs into the bank, in exchange for the stack the order does need — so you can keep clicking until everything is gathered. Items any open order asks for, and your hearthstone, are never swapped out; if every stack in your bags is needed, the click tells you so instead.
  • The addon's saved data is much smaller. Bank contents were being kept twice — once in the current compact form and once in the old form nothing reads any more — and the old copy was more than half of the file on a large guild. It is removed the first time you log in on this version, and every window, tooltip and search reads from the one copy that remains. A big saved file is what makes logging in slow, so this is the difference you may notice.
  • Each bank character's contents are published once more the first time its bank is opened on this version, even if nothing in it changed, so that bank's tab may briefly go red for your guildmates once while the fresh copy reaches them. After that first scan it is quiet again.
  • Catching up on a bank now sends only what changed. When a bank character deposits or withdraws a few things, everyone who already held the previous copy receives just those few changes instead of the whole bank again — a fraction of the size, so a busy guild's updates arrive faster and use far less of the chat channel. Each update is checked against the bank character's own fingerprint of the result before it is applied, so a copy can never end up subtly different from the real bank; if a check fails, the full contents are fetched instead. Someone who has been away a long time, or has never seen that bank, still receives the full contents as before. Both sides need this version.
  • Removed: two developer-only commands that compared the old and new copies, since there is only one copy now.
  • Every cancelled request's date now has a soft, gently pulsing glow, so cancelled orders stand out in the list at a glance. If a reason was given, mouse over the date and it is in the tooltip, as it always was — nobody was finding it there. If the pulse is not for you, switch it off on the Appearance tab of the settings; the reason stays in the tooltip either way.
  • The Requests, Search, Mailbox and Donations windows now carry the same bottom status bar as the main window — the sync activity on the right, and the bank-update line in the middle — and are titled the same way ("TOG Bank v1.5.0 - Requests"), so the windows read as one addon.
  • The Requests tab of the Guild Bank window is now in the same striped-row style as the Browse, Bankers and Log tabs — the same row height, font and spacing, so the four tabs look like one window. The list scrolls instead of paging, so the page arrows are gone; the buttons at the right of each row are now small icons, and mousing over one names it. Because the columns fit a smaller space, the window can be made a good deal narrower than before.
  • The minimap button now opens the Guild Bank window. The old Inventory window (a tab per bank character) is /togbank legacy — bare /togbank still opens it too — and /togbank browse is gone. Shift-click the minimap button for the options, as before.
  • Several orders for the same person can go in one mail. Click Fulfill on each of their requests in turn — each one is added to the mail already open for them, up to the twelve attachments a mail holds — then press Send once, and every order on it is marked filled. The Fulfill Oldest envelope does the same on its own: after picking the oldest order it adds that person's other orders you can fill from your bags to the same mail — including ones that need a stack split. One click does every split at once, each into its own free bag slot (it tells you if you need to make room first), the next attaches everything, and the last sends the one mail.
  • Completed and cancelled requests are kept for 14 days instead of 30. TOGTools keeps the long history.
  • Mousing over items is lighter in a guild with many bank characters. The "Bankers:" lines on an item tooltip used to be worked out by reading through every bank character's entire contents on every hover; each bank now keeps a small per-item tally that is looked up directly and refreshed only when that bank's contents change.
  • Opening the Send Mail tab on a bank character now opens the Guild Bank window on its Requests tab, rather than the old standalone Requests window.
  • The main window's "How It Works" help, and the officers' guild note on it, live on the Guild Bank window's ?.

Fixed

  • A bank character's own counts could show doubled — three Archaic Defenders reading as six, and most of that bank with it. When a viewer character on the same account received that bank's contents from the guild, the copy was kept beside the bank character's own scan, and the two were added together from then on. A scan now replaces the received copy instead of sitting next to it, and any saved data already in that state is repaired the first time you log in, with a chat line saying how many banks it fixed. Nothing to wipe. Guildmates who hold the doubled version see it corrected when the next scan reaches them; their bank log records that correction once, as a withdrawal of the phantom copies.
  • Hiding or showing an item — or any scan that did not open the bank — could take three minutes to reach the guild, and until it did the bank character's own contents could not be handed to anyone who asked ("it's never syncing"). The addon was waiting for every guildmate who had offered that bank to answer a question, and a guildmate on the previous version can offer but never answer, so the wait ran to its time limit every time. Such a guildmate now counts as answered, and the update goes out as soon as the guildmates who can answer have.
  • A Lua error ("invalid key to 'next'") could appear repeatedly in a guild where many people were still on the previous version, as their check-ins were turned away.
  • A bank could read as out of date — on every client, its own included — when nothing newer existed. Guildmates still on the previous version can announce a bank with a made-up "published" time later than the real one (a bug that version has and this one fixed), and that announcement was believed over the bank character's own. Worse, the bank character's own client then thought another computer had published a newer copy, and held back its next rescan while it tried to fetch a copy that was never there. Announcements from a client that cannot hand its contents over are now ignored entirely: a tab only goes red when someone who can actually supply a newer copy has one.
  • In a guild where most people were still on the previous version, every bank tab turned red and stayed red — even for banks you already held the latest copy of. An older client says "I have newer" for every bank on every cycle, because its way of comparing always disagrees; that claim turned the tab red, and the check that would have found it empty was skipped once older clients stopped being asked for anything. A claim from a client that cannot hand its contents over is now ignored entirely, so tabs only go red when someone who can actually supply a newer copy says they have one.
  • Guildmates still on the previous version were quietly blocking everyone who had updated. This version changed how bank contents are handed over, and an older client answers the handover with something this version no longer understands — so it held one of a bank character's three transfer slots for half a minute and then went to the back of the queue, over and over. On a busy evening every slot was taken by guildmates who could never finish, and the ones who could were left waiting at the end of a queue that never reached them. Every guildmate already announces which version they are running; that is now read, so an older client is politely turned away at once and is no longer asked for anything.
  • A bank character could sit marked as out of date for days, even with its bank, bags and mail all freshly opened. Its contents were only ever re-published when something in them changed — so if the guild held a newer copy and yours had not changed since, there was nothing left to trigger the update and it stayed marked out of date indefinitely. Finding out that the guild holds something newer now triggers the update on its own.
  • A bank character could be marked out of date against a version that never existed. Very old saved data carried a bank's contents without a timestamp, and on loading it your game's clock was filled in instead — which then looked to everyone like a copy newer than the one the bank character itself had published, and no amount of rescanning could catch up with it. Nothing invents a timestamp now; a copy that has no proper one is simply re-fetched from a guildmate who has the real thing.
  • The setup instructions told you to enable a setting that does not exist. Both the steps here and the ones in the addon said to turn on "Report bank contents" — there is no such tick. The one next to it is "Report contributions", and all it does is show the "Received X from Y" lines in chat when you open donation mail; it has never had anything to do with whether your bank is shared. The setting that governs that is "Enable for <character>" in the Bank section, and it is on by default. If you have been hunting for a switch to make your bank share, there was not one to find. The instructions now name the real setting, and the troubleshooting steps no longer say the scan happens when you open the bank — it happens when you close it.
  • The Requests list's scrollbar no longer sits on top of the last button in the Actions column — the reopen button was underneath it.
  • The "Highlight needed items" tick on the Requests window now survives a reload. It used to look saved and quietly come back off every time you logged in.
  • The addon package no longer carries an unused library folder or the old changelog archive, so the download is smaller.
  • A bank character that leaves or is removed from the guild while you are online now disappears from your banker list straight away. It used to stay listed — and requestable — until your next login.
  • Sorting by type in the Inventory and Search windows now orders equipment by slot the same way for every bank character. Banks stored in the newer format were being ordered by the slot's internal name instead, and a search spanning one bank in the new format and one in the old could error when sorted by type.
  • The "[WHISPER-SPAM-FIX] Player X is not online" lines that appeared in chat whenever the addon tried to reach someone who had just logged off are gone. That was never something a player needed to see; it now only shows with the roster debug category switched on.
  • Bank characters played from more than one computer — a shared account several people log into from different PCs — are now handled safely. Each computer keeps its own saved copy of that character's bank, so the computer logging in today can hold one that is weeks behind what another computer published. Before, that computer would publish whatever it had the moment it closed a vendor or mailbox: today's bags merged with a vault it had not looked at in weeks, stamped as the newest version, and everyone in the guild adopted it over the correct one. Now that character's own tab goes red on that computer when the guild holds a newer copy, the tooltip says exactly what to do (open the bank and the mailbox on that character), and nothing scanned there is sent to the guild until both have been opened — at which point it publishes the complete, current contents and the tab goes yellow again. Before publishing, that computer first fetches the guild's newer copy from whoever holds it, purely to compare against — it is never kept — so what it sends out is just the changes made on that computer, and the bank log records only those as that character's moves rather than repeating what the other computer already did. If nobody can supply the newer copy within a few minutes it publishes anyway. A character that is only ever played from one computer sees no difference, other than a scan made in the first minute after logging in being sent out a minute later.
  • A bank tab now goes red the moment a guildmate replies that they hold a newer copy than yours, and stays red until that copy arrives. Since the last version those replies no longer carry the version itself, so the tab was staying yellow until the data actually landed — the addon was clearing red well and setting it badly. Hover the tab to see who said they have newer. If the guildmate turns out to hold nothing newer after all, the tab goes back to yellow; if they never answer, it stays red for the next check-in to settle.
  • A guildmate holding a newer copy of a bank than the one you asked for refused to send it, over and over, while sitting idle. If a bank character rescanned in the few seconds between you learning a version and asking for it, the request named the old version and the holder answered "not that one" — and your request could not learn the new version while it was in flight. Holders now send what you asked for or anything newer, refusing only when their own copy is older; and a request in flight picks up a newer announcement instead of ignoring it.
  • A bank character's own copies were sometimes never delivered to a guildmate who asked for them. The bank character would acknowledge the request and then ignore the follow-up asking for the data, so the asker waited out a three-minute timeout and tried someone else — and for a bank only that character had ever scanned, there was nobody else. The same drop happened when the follow-up arrived late on a busy evening. Both are answered now: the acknowledgement reserves the send, a late follow-up is served when there is room, and when there is none the asker is told at once and moves on instead of waiting three minutes. A guildmate who accepts a request after you have already been served elsewhere is now told to stand down, rather than holding a place for you for half a minute.
  • A request the bank could not fill showed the "go and get it from the bank" icon even when the bank did not hold the item at all — a recipe that had already been mailed out, for instance. The icon now says the item was not found in your bags, bank or mail.
  • A request could show as "Item 7969" instead of the item's name, for everyone, for as long as the request lived. It happened when the person placing the request had an item their game had not loaded the name for yet: the placeholder was saved into the request and shared as-is. Requests now look the name up again whenever they are shown, so an old one like that names itself the moment any client can, and a new request is never saved with a placeholder when the name is available. Bank contents that the offline item database does not cover also now pick up the name from your game when it has it, rather than showing the number.
  • A bank character logging in while in a raid group could publish a bags-only scan as if the guild had confirmed nothing newer existed — the addon's own check-in is paused in a raid, so nobody had actually been asked. The check now stays open until the addon can really reach the guild (or its usual time limit passes), the same as any other unanswered check-in.
  • A request from a guildmate whose addon is too old to see the current bank contents now shows their version in amber beside their name, and the fulfil icon's tooltip explains it: they requested from an old copy of your bank, the item may be long gone, and they need to update. Before, such a request looked like any other and the icon could only say the item was not found. The addon now remembers which version each guildmate was last seen on, so the mark shows even when that guildmate is offline — which is usually when you are looking at their order.
  • When the only guildmate holding a newer copy of a bank is on an addon version that cannot send it, the Bankers tab now says so in grey ("Newer copy unreachable", with who and which version on hover) instead of reading "Current". It goes red as soon as someone on the current version has the copy.
  • The Fulfill Oldest tooltip and two status lines used arrow and check-mark symbols the game's fonts cannot draw, which showed as empty boxes. They are plain text now.
  • A saved window size smaller than the window's minimum — from an older version, or a smaller UI scale — is now raised to the minimum when the window opens, so it can never open too small to drag.
  • Items with a random suffix — "Spiked Club of the Bear", "Warmonger's Greaves of the Whale" — showed only the base name everywhere: two different clubs read as the same row when browsing, and a request for one of them reached the bank character as plain "Spiked Club", so the wrong variant could be sent or the right one thought missing. The full name now shows on the Browse and Search tabs, in the bank log, and on every request — including requests placed before this update.
  • Cancelling stale requests with the broom reported how many it cancelled for a split second and then replaced the message with the request count. The message stays now.

v1.4.1 - Collect From The Bank, Faster Sync

Everyone in your guild should update. The way bank characters announce and offer their contents has changed again and a guildmate still on v1.4.0 will simply stop hearing offers from anyone who has updated — nothing breaks for them, they just stop getting newer copies until they update. One thing to do once after updating: open and close each bank character's bank once, so its contents are published in the current format — until then that bank's tab stays red for everyone. The first bank account to do that (or simply to log in, if it already scanned on v1.4.0) numbers every bank character in the guild in one go, and everyone else picks the list up automatically.

New

  • When a bank character opens their bank, everyone online who is behind on it now has the update within seconds. Before, you had to wait for your own next check-in — up to ten minutes — and even then the reply could get lost behind a busy bank character's other transfers. Now a bank character announcing a new version is treated as the offer itself, and you ask for it straight away.
  • Every bank character now has a permanent four-digit number, assigned automatically and the same on everyone's screen. /togbank roster shows the number beside each bank character. Nothing to set up: the first bank account to log in on this version numbers the roster, and everyone else picks the list up from the next message they hear. A new bank character gets the next number the moment its bank is first scanned; a bank character that leaves and comes back keeps its old one.
  • The fulfil button now collects from your bank as well. Open your bank and keep clicking it: each click pulls out what an order still needs, and if the stack is bigger than the order it puts the spare straight back. Gather everything at the bank, walk to a mailbox, then keep clicking the same button to send it. One button, and it does the right thing for wherever you are standing — the game never lets you have a bank and a mailbox open at the same time.

Changed

  • Sync messages are a fraction of their former size. The list every member sends the guild at login and every ten minutes is about a fifth of what it was, and a "I have newer copies of these" reply — which used to be several messages — is now a single one of about a hundred bytes. Less guild-channel traffic for everyone, and the messages that matter get through a busy bank character far sooner.
  • Before asking anyone for a bank's data, the addon now asks the few people who said they have it which version they hold, and takes it from whoever has the newest — the bank character itself if it answers. If everyone holding the newest copy is busy, you wait your turn for the newest rather than taking an older copy from someone idle.
  • A red bank tab now means exactly one of two things, and hovering it tells you which: either that bank's contents are from before the current version and will stay red until its owner opens their bank once, or someone in the guild has a newer copy than you and it is on its way. Red used to be decided by whether the last number a guildmate happened to mention matched yours — which could not tell newer from older, took minutes to settle, and painted banks red that were perfectly current. It is now decided by which copy was published later, it updates the moment anyone mentions a newer copy, and it turns yellow the instant that copy arrives. Your own bank can never be turned red by something another guildmate says about it.
  • Answering a guildmate's "what do you have?" with "I have newer" is now a single comparison instead of a calculation, which is what lets the above be fast.
  • The short back-and-forth between two players — "can I have this bank?", "yes", "you're third in line" — now travels on a fast lane of its own, separate from the bulk bank data. A request is answered within a second or two even while the other player is busy sending banks to someone else; before, that answer could sit for many seconds behind the larger messages.
  • The manual /togbank sync announcement moved off that fast lane so it can never delay those answers.
  • The /togbank share help text and the in-game setup instructions said sharing happened "every 3 minutes". It is every ten minutes, and immediately whenever a bank is scanned; the instructions now say so and tell you to look for your bank character's number in /togbank roster.

Fixed

  • A reply that arrived a little late — after the addon had stopped waiting for replies — was thrown away, so a bank could sit red for ten minutes until the next check-in happened to catch it. Late replies are acted on now.
  • One reply from a guildmate could set off a burst of requests to the whole guild for banks that nobody actually had a newer copy of — twenty-six at once was seen — each one timing out. The addon now asks only when someone has genuinely published something newer than you hold.
  • The developer diagnostic for "is this bank up to date?" could disagree with the tab colour it was meant to explain. They now use the same rule.
  • Bank tabs stayed red after the data had actually arrived, until some unrelated later message happened to repaint them. They now repaint on arrival.
  • A bank whose contents had not changed since before this version could never be brought up to date: because the contents matched, every check said "you already have this" and nothing was ever asked for, so the tab stayed red with no way to clear it. It is now requested the first time its owner is seen on the new version.
  • Data sent by a guildmate still on the previous version arrived with its contents and publish time intact but lost its version marker on the way in, so the tab read as out of date even though it had just been updated. The marker is now kept.
  • Asking a bank character for a bank whose contents had not changed — only its version marker had — got the answer "you already have this", so the new marker never arrived and the tab stayed red even though the request itself had gone out. The bank character now answers based on the version you actually hold, not on whether the contents happen to match.
  • Syncing could grind to a halt in a busy guild. A character sends to three people at a time; anyone beyond that was told "busy" and went off to ask someone else — and a request that was accepted but then turned out to need nothing kept its place for over three minutes, so bank characters were "busy" to everyone, everyone kept re-asking, and the re-asking made it worse. Now a place is given back the moment there is nothing to send, and instead of "busy" you are told your place in the queue: if someone else has the same bank you ask them, and if not you simply wait your turn. Nothing is dropped.
  • Bank data now downloads from several guildmates at the same time instead of three at a time in a fixed order, and offers that could not improve what you already hold are no longer accepted — so the bank you are actually missing is fetched first rather than waiting behind twenty that are already current.
  • Each request carried a full list of what you already hold — several kilobytes — that the sender no longer uses. It now carries only the version markers, a few hundred bytes.
  • A bank character never answered a guildmate's "what do you have?" with their own bank — only with copies of other people's. If that character was the only one online holding the current version, nobody could get it except by asking them directly, and the addon only asks one bank character at a time. They now answer with their own bank too.
  • A bank character sharing their bank now tells everyone the current version straight away; before, the share coloured the tab but the version itself only registered when you next asked directly.
  • A guildmate could advertise a version of a bank they were not actually able to send — an older copy that had picked up a version marker without the data behind it. Everyone else then asked for it, received nothing, and asked again on the next cycle. Only a version that can genuinely be delivered is advertised or answered with now.
  • A version marker that had been damaged by a guildmate on the previous version — passed through as a number and losing its last few digits on the way — could be re-shared as a version that never matched anything, so the bank it belonged to could never read as up to date. Damaged markers are now recognised and ignored.
  • Walking away from the bank part-way through collecting for an order — and then using, mailing or banking the stack you had pulled — left the button stuck saying it was "waiting for the stack to reach your bags" at every bank from then on. Closing the bank now clears it, and it gives up looking after a few tries rather than forever.
  • A fresh install — or a database you had just cleared — could error the first time a bank character answered it, before it had asked for anything, so nothing ever downloaded. Two bank characters in the same guild and a stale copy from a third could also wrestle over which version was "newest", with the loser's tab going red and the addon re-requesting data it could not use.
  • Hovering an item could list a character who is no longer a bank character. Once someone's bank marker is taken off their note they stop being a bank, but the addon keeps their last known contents on file — deliberately, so putting the marker back does not force everything to sync again. The tooltip was reading that stored list without checking who is still a bank, so it kept naming them. It now checks, and reloading was never going to help because nothing was out of date. Bank characters marked view-only are still listed, as intended.
  • Turning on a debug category could fail with "Unknown debug category", and the list you were offered belonged to a different addon entirely. If you have another addon installed that uses the same internal names, it was overwriting this one's list — so a category like BANK looked as though it did not exist, exactly when you were trying to use it to work out what was wrong. Each addon now keeps its own list to itself, in both directions.
  • The performance report listed operations that no longer exist, showing them as zero. A zero there looked like "this ran and cost nothing" when it actually meant "this cannot happen any more", which is misleading in the one place you go to find out why something is slow. The report now lists only what is genuinely measured.
  • How many bags and bank bags to look through was written into the code as a fixed number rather than asked of the game. On Classic Era it happened to be close enough that nothing broke, but it is the kind of number that differs between game versions — and if it were ever too small, a whole bag would quietly go missing from a scan with nothing to tell you. Every place that walks your bags now asks the game.

v1.4.0 - Guild Roster Overhaul

Everyone in your guild needs this version. The way bank contents are shared has changed and is not compatible with older versions — until a bank character updates, players on this version cannot read their contents. Nothing is lost in the meantime, and the addon will tell you by name who still needs to update.

New

  • You can now set how see-through each window is. A new Appearance tab in the options has a slider for every window — Inventory, Search, Requests, Donations and the Mail viewer — so you can make the ones you leave open fade into the background and keep the rest solid. Only the window frame fades: item icons, stack counts and text stay perfectly readable at any setting, and a window you drag transparent still moves and closes normally. Changes take effect straight away on a window that is already open, and there is a "Reset All Windows to Solid" button if you overdo it. Note that 100% is now properly solid — the window art was always slightly see-through before and could not be made fully opaque; if you liked that lighter look, nudge the slider down a little. Thanks to Vishiswaz for the request.

Changed

  • Removed: the old way of sharing bank contents is gone entirely, and with it the bug that kept corrupting item data. To save bandwidth, the addon used to strip the item links out of an update and let the receiving player rebuild them — guessing, item by item, whether the person on the other end would be able to. That guess is about somebody else's game client, and when it was wrong the item came back wrong. Bank contents now travel as plain numbers and the item is rebuilt from a shipped item database, so there is no link to strip and nothing to guess. Everyone in your guild needs this version. Until a bank character updates, players on the new version will not see their contents, and vice versa — it sorts itself out as people update, and nothing is lost in the meantime. If someone in your guild is still on an older version, you will now be told so plainly rather than simply seeing nothing from them.
  • Two addons are now installed alongside this one: LibGuildRoster and AceCommQueue. Guild-roster handling and message queueing moved into shared libraries used across the TOG addons, so a fix in either benefits all of them at once. AceCommQueue was previously bundled inside TOGBankClassic and had fallen behind its own newer releases; as a separate addon it stays current. Both install automatically through the CurseForge app — you do not need to fetch either yourself.
  • The setup steps told you to open your bank, when what actually starts a scan is closing it. Your bank contents are read when you shut the bank window, not when you open it — and opening your bags on their own has never started a scan. The instructions in several places said otherwise, so anyone following them exactly could sit with the bank open wondering why nothing had been shared. The wording is corrected everywhere, and the in-game message you get when there is nothing to compare now tells you to open and close the bank.

Fixed

  • Officers: the guild-wide reset command now works the way it is documented. It is meant to be /togbank dev wipeall, and that spelling was being refused — the command had been filed in the wrong place internally, so it answered only to /togbank wipeall. That put a command which resets the database for every online guild member one word closer to hand than intended. It now sits behind dev as documented.
  • Updating the addon no longer cuts you off from guildmates who have not updated yet. We corrected how the addon fingerprints a bank character's contents, so that swapping one random-suffix item for another is actually noticed. That fingerprint is compared between players, so changing it would have meant an updated client and an out-of-date one could never agree again — each convinced the other was stale, re-syncing endlessly, and the whole guild would have had to update together on the same day. The addon now carries both the old and the new fingerprint and uses whichever the two of you share.
  • A completed update could be reported as a failure fifteen seconds later. When bank contents arrived successfully, the addon stored them and stopped — without ever marking the transfer as finished. The countdown that watches for a stalled transfer kept running, and when it ran out it declared the transfer dead and moved on to somebody else, even though it had worked perfectly. It also meant each completed transfer used up one of the addon's limited transfer slots permanently, so syncing got slower the longer you played.
  • An update could be given up on while it was actually working. When everyone holding a bank character's contents was busy, the addon waited and planned to try again. If somebody freed up in the meantime it went ahead with them — but the old plan to try again was still running, and when it came around it declared the whole thing a failure, even though the update in progress was fine. This also meant the same request could be sent twice. Superseded attempts are now dropped properly, while a genuine retry still happens as before.
  • An update for a bank character could be cancelled part-way through by an earlier attempt for the same character. When the addon asked for a bank character's contents and then asked again a few seconds later — which happens on its own when you open your bank, or when it catches up after you log in — the first attempt was still counting down in the background. When it ran out of time it cancelled whatever was happening at that moment, which by then was the second attempt, and reported it as having timed out. The result was an update that stopped for no visible reason and had to be retried. Only one countdown is now kept per character.
  • "Highlight needed items" did nothing at all if you opened your bags in the wrong order. The addon worked out which bag window to dim by counting, which only matched reality when you opened your backpack first and then each bag in order. Open a bag on its own, or open them out of order, and it was looking at the wrong window entirely — so the tick box turned on and absolutely nothing happened, with no error to explain it. Worse, with two bags open the wrong way round it could dim slots in one bag using the size of another. It now asks the game which window is showing which bag, the same way the game itself does, so the order you open things in no longer matters. This also fixes bank bags dimming the slot at the opposite end of the bag from the one actually holding the item.
  • Items sitting in a bank character's mailbox could go missing for everyone else. If the game could not give the addon a full item link for a mail attachment — which happens routinely — that item was quietly thrown away by everyone who received the update, and rescanning never fixed it because the same thing happened every time. Those items now arrive. This is a second, separate cause of "the banker's mail is missing" from the one fixed earlier in this version; that one was about recording mail, this one was about sending it.
  • A bank character's own tooltip could show a smaller count than everybody else saw. Hovering the same item on the bank character and on a normal character gave two different numbers — for example 68 against 71 — and the bank character was always the one that was short. The cause was mail. Your bank vault can only be read while you are standing at a banker, so to avoid wiping a stored vault the addon skipped saving anything at all when you were away from one — which also threw away the mail and bag counts it had just read. Since you almost never open your mailbox standing at a banker, mail could never make it in. The vault and your bags and mail are now stored separately, so being away from a banker simply leaves the vault untouched instead of cancelling the whole update.
  • Your guild bank totals are no longer replaced by older, wrong ones. A bank character's contents are stamped by that character when they scan, and every other player now carries that stamp along unchanged. Previously each client worked out its own version of it, so a figure that had travelled through several players could claim to be newer than the bank character's own — and older contents quietly overwrote current ones. The most visible form was a bank character going offline and their totals then changing to something stale. Whichever copy was genuinely scanned most recently now wins, every time.
  • Bank contents now use about 85% less data to send. Item information travels as compact numbers and your own client rebuilds the item from them, instead of every item's full link being sent to everyone. Measured on a live guild across 227 items: 19,740 bytes down to 2,873.
  • Much less background chatter between guild members. Because every client used to recalculate that stamp — including every time anyone simply looked at a bank — the figures never settled, and members constantly re-sent each other data they already had. On a busy guild that was a lot of traffic for no benefit. A bank character's contents are now stamped once, when they are scanned, and nothing else touches it.
  • A completed order no longer stays open on everyone else's list. Filling, cancelling and deleting an order now reliably reach every other member, so two people are far less likely to fill the same request.
  • Random-suffix gear such as "of the Tiger" and "of the Monkey" is no longer mixed up. Two versions of the same base item held by one bank character used to be shown as a single entry, with the two stock counts added together and only one version's tooltip — so you could see a count that no version actually had, and the order you placed could arrive as the wrong one. Each version is now listed separately with its own count, and an order remembers exactly which one you asked for.
  • Requesting a specific version of an item now works even when your item database has not loaded it. Previously, if your client could not look the item up, the version you picked was quietly forgotten and any matching item could be sent instead.
  • The separate debug chat tab no longer errors on some setups. Depending on one of your client's settings, creating or removing the tab could throw an error rather than simply doing nothing.
  • The debug tab now tells you when it will not show anything yet. It used to say output would appear there even when debug logging was still switched off, so it looked broken when it was only unconfigured. It now names exactly what to turn on.
  • You are told if the addon cannot register for guild messages. There is a limit on how many addons can use guild communication at once, and if you are over it TOGBank simply received nothing — no sync, no error, no clue. It now says so plainly.
  • Counts saved before the mail fix are no longer trusted over complete ones. Records saved by an older version hold your bags and vault but no mail, so they read low. The addon now marks each saved record with what it covers, and falls back to the older complete figure until that character is scanned again — which happens by itself the next time they open and close their bank. Nothing to run and nothing to reset.
  • The addon now notices when guild members come and go, as it happens. It was supposed to watch for the "X has come online" messages and keep its own list of who is around — but a fault meant that watcher never actually ran, in every version ever released. The addon only found out who was online when the game handed it a full guild refresh. In between, it could try to send an order to someone who had already logged off, and it was slower to notice a banker coming online with items you were waiting for. It now tracks this properly and immediately.
  • Bank characters who leave the guild no longer linger. Removing a character from your bank list, or from the guild, could leave a ghost entry behind that showed as permanently waiting for data. Those cannot occur any more.
  • The inventory window no longer gets stuck on "Loading items...". If any single item in your guild bank failed to load, the addon stopped waiting for the whole batch — so one bad item hid everything and the window sat on the loading message indefinitely. Now a problem item is skipped and the rest still appear, and if something never loads at all the addon gives up after a few seconds and shows you what it has rather than waiting forever.
  • The status bar now shows when your character is sending inventory to someone. The bar along the bottom of the Inventory window has a "Tx" readout meant to show sends in progress — it counted the wrong thing and was stuck on zero, so it stayed blank no matter how busy the addon was. There was no way to tell the difference between "nothing is happening" and "the indicator is broken". It now shows the real number, turning amber while sends are in flight and red when you are at the limit. Sending itself was never affected — only your view of it.
  • Bank contents were being constantly re-checked and re-sent between guild members, for no reason. Each bank character's contents carry a short fingerprint, used to tell at a glance whether two players are holding the same thing. That fingerprint was supposed to be written once, by the bank character itself. Instead almost every player recalculated it — whenever anyone asked about a bank, whenever certain replies arrived, and when older saved data was upgraded. Because each player worked it out slightly differently, players who held identical contents kept concluding they disagreed, and asked each other for the data again. On a busy guild that is a lot of chatter with nothing behind it. The fingerprint is now written once, by the bank character whose bank it is, and everyone else passes it along untouched.
  • A bad fingerprint could spread from player to player and replace good data. The same recalculation meant a fingerprint invented by one player was sent on to the next, stored as if it were genuine, and passed on again — so a wrong value could travel the guild and overwrite correct bank contents, most visibly after the bank character had logged off and was no longer around to correct it. Players no longer accept a fingerprint from anyone except the bank character it belongs to.
  • Two different bank contents that happened to look identical are no longer mistaken for each other. The fingerprint now includes when the contents were recorded, so a genuine change is never mistaken for "nothing happened". This is also what lets the addon tell which of two copies is actually the newer one.
  • A bank character's contents could be replaced by an older copy, so items that were really there stopped showing. When several guild members each hold a copy of the same bank character, they pass it around between them. The addon applied whichever copy arrived last and never checked how old it was — so somebody logging in with a days-old copy could overwrite the up-to-date one for everyone, and there was nothing to say it had happened. It could not check, because a bank character's contents were sent without any record of when they were taken; each player stamped them with the time they received them, which meant a copy passed along third-hand looked newer than the original the further it travelled. Contents now carry the time they were actually scanned, and an older copy is ignored rather than applied.
  • The addon now tells you if the game has no room left for it to talk to your guild. WoW allows only a limited number of addon conversations at once, and once that limit is reached any further ones are simply refused. TOGBank could be one of the refused ones — and when that happened nothing was said. No error, no warning, just a guild bank that never updated, with nothing anywhere to explain why. It now checks, and if it has been shut out it says so plainly and tells you that disabling another addon and reloading will fix it. If everything is fine, as it will be for almost everyone, you will never see this.
  • The addon notices when the game refuses to send something. Occasionally the client declines to send an addon message. Previously that was reported back as success, so the addon believed your guild had received an update they never got — most importantly changes to orders, which could leave different members seeing different lists with nothing to explain why. Failed sends are now retried, and if they still fail you are told, instead of the addon quietly assuming it worked.

v1.3.2 (2026-08-03) - ElvUI & Baganator Item Highlighting

The "Highlight needed items" option now works for ElvUI and Baganator users.

New

  • "Highlight needed items" works with ElvUI. If you use ElvUI's bags, ticking the box did nothing at all — the addon only knew how to dim items in Bagnon and in the default Blizzard bags. It now dims unneeded items directly in ElvUI's bag and bank windows, using ElvUI's own shading so it looks native and doesn't interfere with ElvUI's search box. Unticking the box restores everything to normal. Works the same on Classic Era and TBC.
  • "Highlight needed items" works with Baganator. If you use Baganator for your bags, ticking the box did nothing — the addon only knew how to mark items in Bagnon and in the default Blizzard bags. Items you need for open orders now get a gold marker in the top-left corner of the icon. Note this looks different to the Bagnon and default-bag versions, which instead dim everything you don't need: Baganator only lets addons add corner markers, so that is the form it takes there. You can move or remove the marker from Baganator's own customise window, where it appears as "TOGBank: needed for an order".

Changed

  • Bagnon users are unaffected. If you run Bagnon underneath ElvUI with ElvUI's own bag replacement turned off, highlighting keeps using Bagnon exactly as before.

Please report anything odd with ElvUI or Baganator. Support for both was written against those addons' own published interfaces, but neither is installed on the development machine, so this release is the first time either has run for real. Both are built to fail quietly — if something is wrong they fall back to the previous behaviour rather than throwing errors — so if highlighting doesn't do what's described above, please report it and say which bag addon you use. Bagnon and default-bag users are on the same code path as before and unaffected either way.

v1.3.1 (2026-08-02) - Banker Inventory Never Scanned

Fixes a bank character's own items never being recorded, leaving their tab permanently empty.

Changed

  • Easier to diagnose if scanning ever stops. When the addon decides not to scan, it can now tell you why (for example, the character isn't marked as a bank, or scanning is switched off for them) instead of doing nothing silently. Turn on the new BANK category in the debug options if you are troubleshooting.

Fixed

  • Scanning can no longer switch itself off behind your back. Depending on timing at login, a bank character's "enable scanning" setting could end up unset — which silently stopped their bags and bank from being recorded — and the Bank section could be missing from the addon's options entirely, so the "Enable for <character>" tick box wasn't there to turn on. The setting now defaults to on and the options section is registered reliably. Affects Classic Era as well as TBC.
  • An empty tab no longer says "Loading items..." forever. A character with nothing recorded yet showed a loading message that never went away, so an empty record looked identical to the addon being stuck. Empty tabs now tell you what is actually going on — and, for your own character, what to do about it.

v1.3.0 (2026-08-02) - TBC Client Support

TOGBankClassic now installs and runs on The Burning Crusade clients as well as Classic Era.

New

  • The addon now loads on TBC. Previously only a Classic Era build was published, so TBC players saw the addon flagged as out of date and had to force-load it. There is now a proper TBC build with the same features, same commands and same interface — install it the usual way and it just works.

Fixed

  • No longer flagged "out of date" on Classic Era. The Era build was still marked for the previous game patch, so it showed up greyed out in the AddOns list until you ticked "Load out of date AddOns". It now matches the current patch.

v1.2.1 (2026-07-30) - Settings Panel Open Fix

Fixes the settings panel refusing to open after a recent client update.

Fixed

  • The settings panel opens again. Clicking the gear icon in the bank window, or picking Settings from the minimap button, threw an error and nothing opened. A recent change to how the game's Settings window is addressed meant the addon was asking for its options page the old way. Both buttons now take you straight to the TOGBankClassic options page.
